# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct. The full text is available at:

> <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Summary

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### Expected behavior

- Be kind, empathetic, and respectful.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Prioritize what is best for the overall community.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers privately via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Dmitrze/repo-trust/security/advisories/new) (the same private channel used for security reports). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers will follow the [Contributor Covenant Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) when determining the consequences of any violation: Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and any other interaction connected to the project — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
